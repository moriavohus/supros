Selected cases: what the client asked for, what we did and what came out of it.

Gas processing plant, Atyrau region

Task
Thermal calculation and take-off for steam tracing lines.
Scope
12 km of piping, hot insulation with aluminium cladding.
Result
Heat loss cut by a quarter, materials shipped from stock.

Cryogenic storage, Caspian coast

Task
Cold service insulation for LNG receiving equipment.
Scope
Cellular glass with vapour barrier, 1 800 m² of surface.
Result
No condensation on the cold bridges after the first winter.

Repair scope planned without stopping the neighbouring unit.
